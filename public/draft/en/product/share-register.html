What a share register must contain

For a company that is not a CSD-registered company (avstämningsbolag), chapter 5, section 5 of the Swedish Companies Act says the register must show:

  • the number of each share
  • each shareholder's name, personal or company ID number and postal address
  • the class of each share, if the company has more than one
  • whether share certificates have been issued
  • whether the share is subject to a restriction, such as pre-emption or consent clauses

Shares are listed in numerical order. Changes must be entered straight away and dated (chapter 5, section 9).

Why spreadsheet registers go wrong

A register in Excel is a copy of reality that someone has to update by hand. With many companies and several people touching the files, it is easy to end up with the wrong version, share numbers that don't add up or a transfer that never got recorded.

In Custora the register isn't a separate document. It is built from the company's transactions, so it always matches what actually happened.

Frequently asked questions

Can a share register be kept digitally?
Yes. Chapter 5, section 2 of the Swedish Companies Act says the register shall be kept by automated processing. Companies that are not CSD-registered may also keep it in a bound book or a secure loose-leaf or card system. A digital register follows the main rule.
Who is responsible for the share register?
In companies that are not CSD-registered, the board is responsible for keeping, storing and making the register available (chapter 5, section 7). The company is the data controller for the personal data in it (chapter 5, section 4).
How long must the register be kept?
For as long as the company exists and for at least ten years after it is dissolved (chapter 5, section 3).
Who can see the share register?
In companies that are not CSD-registered, the register must be available at the company to anyone who wants to see it. If it is kept digitally, the company must be able to show a current printout to anyone who asks (chapter 5, section 10).
Does Bolagsverket hold our share register?
No. The company keeps its own register, and in companies that are not CSD-registered the board is responsible for it (chapter 5, section 7).
