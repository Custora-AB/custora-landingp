What the day looks like

With ten, fifty or two hundred companies, ownership ends up scattered: registers in Excel, minutes in Word, agreements in email and a group chart someone drew a year ago. Every issue or transfer has to go into several places, and it is rarely clear who updated what last.

In Custora the change is entered once. The register, the group structure and the history are built from it, and the documents are filed with the company they belong to.

Frequently asked questions

Does Custora replace our fund administrator?
No. Custora sits next to your fund administrator and accounting system and holds ownership and governance: registers, structure, owner lists, decisions and signed documents.
Can a share register be kept digitally?
Yes. Chapter 5, section 2 of the Swedish Companies Act says the register shall be kept by automated processing. Companies that are not CSD-registered may also keep it in a bound book or a secure loose-leaf or card system. A digital register follows the main rule.
How long must the register be kept?
For as long as the company exists and for at least ten years after it is dissolved (chapter 5, section 3).
