What the law requires of minutes

  • AGM minutes must be available to shareholders no later than two weeks after the meeting, and a copy sent to any shareholder who asks (Companies Act, chapter 7, section 49).
  • Board minutes are signed by the person who took them and checked by the chair. If the board has several members, one more member chosen by the board also checks them (chapter 8, section 24).
  • Board minutes are kept in numbered order and stored securely (chapter 8, section 26).

Custora keeps minutes per company and in order, with signing you can trace.

Frequently asked questions

How are documents signed?
With BankID. You pick a PDF in the company's data room, choose who signs and follow the status. The signed document is saved back in the same data room.
Who can see the share register?
In companies that are not CSD-registered, the register must be available at the company to anyone who wants to see it. If it is kept digitally, the company must be able to show a current printout to anyone who asks (chapter 5, section 10).
How long must the register be kept?
For as long as the company exists and for at least ten years after it is dissolved (chapter 5, section 3).
