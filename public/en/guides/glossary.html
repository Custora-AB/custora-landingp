Glossary

Terms from Swedish company law, governance and funds, explained briefly and with a reference to the statute where there is one.

Last reviewed 17 November 2026. Section references are to the Swedish Companies Act (2005:551) unless the Swedish AIFM Act (2013:561) is named.

Companies and governance

Share register (aktiebok)
The company's list of its shares and their owners. In companies that are not CSD-registered, the board is responsible for keeping, storing and making it available (Companies Act ch. 5 s. 7). Read more →
Share certificate (aktiebrev)
A certificate of ownership of shares. The company must issue one if a shareholder asks (ch. 6 s. 1). CSD-registered companies may not issue them (ch. 6 s. 10).
Share capital
The capital contributed by shareholders and tied up in the company. In a private limited company it must be at least SEK 25,000 (ch. 1 s. 5).
Payment in kind (apport)
Property other than money used to pay for shares. It must be useful to the company's business, and work or services do not count (ch. 2 s. 6, ch. 13 s. 20). Read more →
CSD-registered company (avstämningsbolag)
A company whose articles say its shares are registered in a central securities register under Act 1998:1479 (ch. 1 s. 10). Most private companies are not CSD-registered.
Authorisation (bemyndigande)
A general meeting decision letting the board decide on a new issue, as far as it can be done without changing the articles (ch. 13 s. 35). Read more →
Transfer restrictions
Clauses in the articles that limit transfers. Consent clause: a transfer needs the company's consent (ch. 4 s. 8). Right of first offer: the share must be offered to others before it is transferred (ch. 4 s. 18). Pre-emption clause: others may buy out a share that has changed hands (ch. 4 s. 27). Read more →
Pre-emption right in a new issue
Shareholders' right to subscribe for new shares in proportion to the shares they own. The main rule in a new issue (ch. 13 s. 1). Read more →
Group, parent and subsidiary
A limited company is the parent of another legal person if, among other things, it holds more than half the votes in it. Parent and subsidiaries together form a group (ch. 1 s. 11). Read more →
Quota value
Each share's portion of the share capital (ch. 1 s. 6).
New share issue
When the company issues new shares for payment. Decided by the general meeting, or by the board under an authorisation or subject to later approval, and filed for registration within six months (ch. 13). Read more →
Per capsulam
A decision taken without the participants meeting, with the minutes sent round for signature. The term is not in the Companies Act; it is a working practice. Read more →
Voting list (röstlängd)
The list of shareholders, proxies and assistants present at a general meeting (ch. 7 s. 29). It goes into or is attached to the minutes (ch. 7 s. 48). Read more →
Subscription list
The document new shares are subscribed on. It must contain the issue decision (ch. 13 s. 13). Read more →
Annual general meeting
The ordinary general meeting, held within six months of the end of the financial year (ch. 7 s. 10). Notice goes out no earlier than six and no later than four weeks before (ch. 7 s. 18). Read more →

Funds and AIF managers

Alternative investment fund (AIF)
A collective investment undertaking that raises capital from a number of investors to invest it under a defined investment policy, and is not a UCITS fund (Swedish AIFM Act ch. 1 s. 2). Read more →
AIF manager
A legal person whose regular business is managing AIFs (ch. 1 s. 3). Authorisation and registration are handled by Finansinspektionen. Read more →
Registered AIF manager
A manager registered with FI instead of authorised. Applies if the funds' assets including leverage do not exceed EUR 100m, or EUR 500m without leverage and no redemption rights for five years (ch. 2 ss. 2–3). Registered managers also report to FI. Read more →
EuVECA
The EU label for European venture capital funds under Regulation (EU) 345/2013. A registered AIF manager that wants to use it must notify FI.
Fidac
Finansinspektionen's reporting system for periodic reporting, used among other things for AIFMD reports. Read more →
Commitment
The amount an investor has promised to put into a fund. It is paid in over time, as the manager makes capital calls. Read more →
Capital call
When the manager calls in part of the investors' commitments, usually split by the size of each investor's commitment. Read more →
NAV (net asset value)
A fund's assets minus its liabilities. Used among other things to express leverage in AIFMD reporting. Read more →
SPV
A company set up for a single purpose, such as holding one property or one investment. A fund can have many SPVs underneath it. Read more →
