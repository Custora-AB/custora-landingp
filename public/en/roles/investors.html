Frequently asked questions

How is my capital call worked out?
By commitment. If your commitment is 10% of the fund's total commitments, your share is 10% of the call.
How do I sign?
With BankID. You receive the document for signing, sign it, and the signed copy is filed with the company.
Who can see the share register?
In companies that are not CSD-registered, the register must be available at the company to anyone who wants to see it. If it is kept digitally, the company must be able to show a current printout to anyone who asks (chapter 5, section 10).
