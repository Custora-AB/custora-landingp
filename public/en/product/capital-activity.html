How a capital call works today

It often starts with a verbal request or a chat message. Then one person opens the spreadsheet, works out each investor's share, writes the notices and follows up payments by email. That works until several funds call capital in the same week at short notice.

How it works in Custora

Choose fund and amount

Enter how much to call and when it is due.

Check the split

Custora splits the amount by commitment before anything goes out.

Send the notices

Each investor gets their own notice with their amount.

Track payments

See who has paid and what is left to call per investor.

Distributions the other way

Distributions work the same way: the amount is split by share and saved in each investor's history. Calls and distributions both run on the owner list already in the system, so nothing is entered twice.

Equalisation when new investors come in

When an investor joins at a later close, Custora works out what they need to pay to catch up with the investors who have already paid in, and includes it in the next call.

Frequently asked questions

How is the split worked out?
By each investor's commitment to the fund, pro rata. You see the split before anything goes out.
Does it work across several funds?
Yes. Each fund has its own owner list, calls and distributions.
Does it replace the fund administrator?
No. Custora sits next to your fund administrator and accounting system and holds ownership and governance: registers, structure, owner lists, decisions and signed documents.
