What the Companies Act requires

  • General meeting minutes: available to shareholders no later than two weeks after the meeting, with a copy sent to any shareholder who asks (chapter 7, section 49).
  • Board minutes: every decision recorded. The minutes are signed by the person who took them and checked by the chair, and if the board has several members, also by one member the board appoints (chapter 8, section 24).
  • Order: board minutes are kept in numbered order and stored securely (chapter 8, section 26).

For a group or a firm with many companies that adds up to hundreds of documents a year, and it is tempting to start each one from a copy of last year's.

Frequently asked questions

Who signs board minutes?
The person who took the minutes signs, and the chair checks them unless the chair took them. If the board has several members, one more member chosen by the board also checks them (chapter 8, section 24).
When must AGM minutes be ready?
They must be available to shareholders no later than two weeks after the meeting (chapter 7, section 49).
How are documents signed?
With BankID. You pick a PDF in the company's data room, choose who signs and follow the status. The signed document is saved back in the same data room.
