The chart that is always one version behind

In most groups the structure chart is a PowerPoint slide. It changes when someone remembers to update it, and when the bank, the auditor or a new investor asks for it, nobody is sure it is right.

Drawn from ownership

In Custora the group tree is drawn from the ownership data already in the system. Form a company or record a transfer between group companies and the tree follows, with the ownership share on every link. Part-owned companies show their share.

When you need the structure

  • Audits and year-end
  • Credit processes with the bank
  • Investor reporting
  • Due diligence on acquisitions and sales

Frequently asked questions

Does the structure update when a company is sold?
Yes. When the transfer is recorded the ownership changes and the tree follows.
Are part-owned companies shown?
Yes. Every link in the tree shows the ownership share, so part-owned companies appear with their share.
