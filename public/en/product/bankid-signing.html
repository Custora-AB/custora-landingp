How it works

Pick a PDF in the company's data room, choose who signs and send it. You can see who has signed and who is missing. Once everyone has signed, the sealed document is saved back in the same data room.

What you can sign

  • Board and general meeting minutes
  • Subscription lists for new issues
  • Share transfer agreements
  • Shareholder agreements and other contracts

Why in the same system

When signing happens in a separate tool, the signed copy ends up wherever the sender keeps things. In Custora it stays with the company it belongs to, next to the register and the minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Which documents can be signed?
PDFs in the company's data room, such as minutes, subscription lists and agreements.
Where does the signed document end up?
In the same data room as the original, with the company it belongs to.
